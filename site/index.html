Your prints.
At a glance.

See print progress, time remaining, temperatures and camera snapshots from your 3D printers. Add a widget, show two printer cameras side by side, or keep a print in your Mac menu bar.

Beta. Only the Bambu Lab H2D and Creality K1 Max have been checked on real hardware so far. If you try another printer, please report from the app whether it works, partly works or doesn't.

Get it on the App Store ↗ How to report a problem ↓

iPhone and iPad: iOS / iPadOS 17+. Free, no account or subscription.
Mac: macOS 14+, joining the same App Store listing, so one download covers both.

Get connected.

Connect to the printer's network or a suitable VPN, add your printer, and allow Local Network access. Explore the built-in demo if you want to look around first.

Supports Bambu Lab LAN, Klipper / Moonraker, OctoPrint, PrusaLink, Duet / RepRapFirmware, Creality K1-series native status, and SDCP v3. Compatibility depends on firmware and the interface your printer exposes.

There are no push notifications. Apple schedules widget refreshes and may delay them. Readings keep their actual timestamps; open the app for a fresh update.

An older notarized Mac build (0.5.1) remains on the releases page and is no longer updated.

Setup and compatibility details ↗

Tell us how it went.

In the app, open Settings and help, then Report a problem. On Mac you can also use Help, then Report a Problem. Say whether a printer works, partly works or doesn't, pick the printer and tick what you checked. No account is needed. After adding a printer, the app also offers Tell the Developer It Works.

Reports can attach a diagnostics file that you preview before sending and can turn off. It lists the app version, device model, printer type, the kind of address, which readings arrived and the last error. It never includes printer names, addresses, serial numbers, access codes, job names, camera images or logs.
