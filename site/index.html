Your prints.
At a glance.

See print progress, time remaining, and temperatures from your supported 3D printers. Add a widget, or keep a print in your Mac menu bar.

Download on the App Store ↗ Download for Mac ↓

App Store: iPhone and iPad, iOS / iPadOS 17+.
Direct Mac download: macOS 14+, Apple silicon and Intel.

Get connected.

Connect to the printer's network or a suitable VPN, add your printer, and allow Local Network access. Explore the built-in demo if you want to look around first.

Supports Bambu Lab LAN, Klipper / Moonraker, OctoPrint, PrusaLink, Duet / RepRapFirmware, Creality K1-series native status, and SDCP v3. Compatibility depends on firmware and the interface your printer exposes.

Apple schedules widget refreshes and may delay them. Readings keep their actual timestamps; open the app for a fresh update.

The direct Mac download remains available while the Mac App Store version awaits approval. Features can differ between the App Store version and the direct Mac release.
